ILEM Mail Automation

Internal email tool of ILEM PS (ilemps.com)

ILEM Mail Automation is a private desktop tool used by the ILEM PS team to manage correspondence in the team's own Gmail and Google Workspace mailboxes: reading incoming messages, sending emails, replying within existing conversations and organizing messages with labels.

The tool runs only on the owner's computer and is used only with mailboxes that belong to the ILEM PS team. It is not offered to the public.
